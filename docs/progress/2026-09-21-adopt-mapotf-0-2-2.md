## Validation

The [v0.2.2 release](https://github.com/Azure/mapotf/releases/tag/v0.2.2)
includes [Azure/mapotf#131](https://github.com/Azure/mapotf/pull/131).
At preparation time, the release asset list is empty.
`.\scripts\Update-AvmPins.ps1 -Mapotf 0.2.2` returned HTTP 404 for
`checksums.txt`; the pin manifest was not changed.

The earlier prototype's 1.11-second warm measurement covered one example
target, not the full AVM transform stage. Do not treat that as verification
of a 50% end-to-end improvement in the released binary. Fresh CI workers
will have a cold cache unless it is restored between jobs.

## Blockers or dependencies

Awaiting publication of the official 0.2.2 binaries and `checksums.txt`.
Keep the 0.2.1 version and hashes together until the refresh script can
retrieve and validate all six replacement hashes; never commit placeholder
hashes or combine the new version with old checksums.
